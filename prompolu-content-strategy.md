## 1. Your niche

**B2B aluminium profile supply for window, door and kitchen fabricators, with an AR sales tool that gives your customers a way to sell.**

- You are the **official STRUGAL distributor** for Fès, Sefrou and the surrounding region (stores on Av. El Wafaa in Fès and Route d'El Menzel in Sefrou), 18+ years in business, delivery across Morocco.
- You sell **raw material, not finished windows**: 11 sliding systems (S70P to S140RP Infinity), casement systems (S53RP+, S46) plus ALUDOORS doors, interior fit-out profiles (kitchens and dressings with integrated handles), and exterior lines (railings, roller shutters, pergolas, cladding).
- **Your customers are menuisiers.** Their customers are homeowners who say "I can't picture it".
- **Your unfair advantage is the 3D/AR tool** (pergola, railing, sliding bay, casement). It's white-labelled to each menuisier, needs no app, loads in about a second on 4G, and returns a complete WhatsApp quote request. I found no competitor in my sample showing anything like it.
- **Your voice** on the site is plain, practical and workshop-friendly: "Quatre étapes, sans formalité inutile."

**Two audiences, one funnel.**
1. **Menuisiers** (buyers). They care about stock, colours, specs, hardware tricks, delivery and not wasting time.
2. **Homeowners** (demand creators). If they ask their menuisier for STRUGAL, the menuisier comes to you. Most of the views in this niche come from this group.

Your content should serve the first group and use the second as a pull-through. Views from homeowners who can't buy from you are nice but they don't pay the bills. **Measure WhatsApp enquiries, not views.**

### Seven patterns

1. **Small accounts go big.** Accounts with 12K to 18K followers hit 1.7M to 1.8M, and some under 500 followers hit 150K. **Follower count isn't the barrier.**
2. **A clever mechanism in under 20 seconds.** "No drilling", "no track", "disappears into the wall", "telescopic". Each shows one surprising mechanism and nothing else.
3. **Kitchens dominate Moroccan aluminium TikTok.** Captions mentioning kitchens have a median of ~30K views (n=80). Doors ~14K, sliding systems ~10K, windows ~2K, pergolas ~2.5K, railings ~2.5K. (Categories overlap and the sample is search-biased.)
4. **Arabic captions get about 4× the reach in Morocco**: median 18.2K (n=85) vs 4.5K (n=103) for non-Arabic. Your 10 videos are all French or hashtag-only.
5. **Less caption is better**: hashtag-only captions have a median of 16.8K vs 6.8K. Captions that put a **phone number in the text** do worse (4.8K vs 13.9K). (Your own winners put the number **on the video**, not in the caption.)
6. **Price and cost content gets saved.** Captions mentioning price/prix/devis: median 12K and one of the highest save rates (0.38% of views, n=30, all niche videos).
7. **City tags in the top quartile**: #casablanca, #rabat, #bouznika, #mohammedia, plus Arabic category tags like #مطابخ_حديثة_المنيوم.

Length is a weak signal: under 10s and over 60s both did better than the 20–35s middle, but the sample is small. Hook strength matters more than length.

## 6. Content ideas

Ranked by how directly your own data or the niche data supports them. **Hooks are drafts in French and Darija; adapt them to how you actually speak.** Technical claims should be double-checked against STRUGAL's sheets before you film. Spec numbers below come from your own site.

### A. Proven for you: stock, trucks, colours (start here)

**1. "Ce camion part pour …"** (the 14.7K format)
- Film: a loaded truck leaving, with the destination city as on-screen text. 10–16s, no talking, phone number overlaid.
- Hook text: *"Ce camion part pour Meknès ce matin"* / *هاد الكاميو غادي لمكناس دابا*
- Make it a **weekly series** with a new city every time. Each video targets a local audience (#meknes, #taza, #oujda) and shows the "delivery across Morocco" promise.

**2. "Toutes les teintes en stock"** (the 8K format, upgraded)
- Film: a smooth walk down the aisle, with each colour appearing as an on-screen label (noir, gris naturel, fauxbois, blanc 9010, gris platine, gris givré). 15–22s.
- Upgrade: end with the same profile in three colours side by side.
- Hook: *"Même profil, 6 teintes. Laquelle choisit ton client ?"* / *نفس البروفيل، 6 ألوان. أي واحد غادي يختار الكليان؟*

**3. "Arrivage"**
- Film: a delivery from STRUGAL being unloaded or a fresh bundle being opened. Satisfying, no talking. 8–12s.
- Hook: *"Nouvel arrivage S88RP"* / *وصلنا S88RP*

### B. Proven for you: the counter tip series (most saves, shares and comments)

**4. "Le truc que tout menuisier devrait savoir"**
- Film: the presenter at the counter holds one profile and one part, shows **one** fix, under 30s. Same camera, same set, every time, so the audience learns to recognise it.
- Hook: *"Une astuce de 20 secondes qui évite les retours de chantier"* / *حيلة د 20 ثانية كتحميك من المشاكل فالورش*
- Topics that fit your range (verify each): dust protection on the flat lock (follow-up to your best tip), where the thermal break sits, glazing thickness limits per system, wheel adjustment on a lift-and-slide, drainage at the bottom rail.
- Ask a question at the end ("Et toi, tu fais comment ?") to lift comments. Your comment counts are 1–11.

**5. "Comparer en main": S70P vs S90RP**
- Film: hold two profiles side by side, point at the thermal barrier. Your site says "comparer en main… apprend en deux minutes ce qu'un catalogue met une heure à expliquer", so film exactly that. 20–30s.
- Hook: *"Où est la rupture de pont thermique ?"* / *فين كاينة قطعة العزل الحراري؟*

**6. Quiz: "Combien de kg ?"**
- Film: a profile close-up, text "S70P, S90RP, S88RP, S110P: lequel porte 400 kg par vantail ?" Reveal at the end. Your site lists: S70P 80 kg, S86RP 140 kg, S90P/S90RP 160 kg, S88 200–300 kg, S110P 400 kg.
- Comment-bait with a built-in answer. Pin a comment with the full list.
- Hook: *"Quel profil porte le plus lourd vantail ?"* / *شمن بروفيل كيحمل أتقل ضلفة؟*

### C. Your unique asset: the 3D/AR tool (nobody else shows this)

**7. "Il hésite ? Montre-lui chez lui"**
- Screen-record on a phone: someone points the camera at a terrace, a pergola appears at 4.20 × 6.00 m, rotates, changes colour. 15–25s. A before/after split with the empty terrace works well.
- Hook: *"Ton client n'arrive pas à se projeter ? Voilà la solution"* / *الكليان ما قادرش يتخيل؟ شوف هاد الحل*
- CTA: "Menuisiers : demandez votre lien" (it's reserved for professional clients, which creates exclusivity).

**8. Speed-run: "Une pergola configurée en 30 secondes"**
- Screen recording with a visible timer. Colour, size, blades, LED. End with the WhatsApp message arriving, which shows the full quote request landing.

**9. Menuisier case studies**
- Invite 3 workshop customers to use their personal link and film themselves with a client. Post it, tag them. They'll repost to their own audience, which is also marketing for you.

### D. The viral "one clever mechanism" format, on your systems

**10. "Disparaît dans le mur"** (S88RP with galandage)
- 8–12s: the sliding leaf slides fully into the wall. No talking. This is the format that got 1.8M to 9M for others.

**11. "Le plus fin du catalogue"** (S86RP, 42 mm at the centre)

### G. Price and ordering (the format with the best save rate)

### H. Relatable and local (untested here; try 3–4 and keep what works)

**22. Menuisier skit**: "Le client veut un 'petit coulissant', il mesure 4 mètres." Darija humour, 15s.
