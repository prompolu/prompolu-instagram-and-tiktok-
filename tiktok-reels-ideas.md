# 20 TikTok & Reels ideas for PROMPOLU

Built from the research in `prompolu-content-strategy.md`: your 10 TikToks, 479 niche videos (188 Moroccan) and the specs on prompolu.com.

**How to read each card**
- **Hook**: the on-screen text for the first 1–2 seconds, in French and Darija. The Darija lines are drafts, so change them to how you actually speak.
- **Shots**: what to film, beat by beat.
- **Why**: the evidence. *Yours* = your own TikTok results. *Niche* = the 479-video sample. *Untested* = a reasonable bet with no data behind it.
- Length is a target, not a rule. Everything is 9:16.

**Rules for every video**
1. Hook text in the first second. Your captions currently say "Retrouvez-nous sur notre site", which gives people no reason to watch.
2. Phone number on the video, not in the caption (your winners did this; niche captions with numbers did worse).
3. Caption: one short line (Arabic or French) plus 3–5 tags: a city, a category and one Arabic tag. Drop #viral and #fyp.
4. Original sound. Your top 3 all used it.
5. Film it, don't animate it. Your filmed videos have a median of 4,546 views, against 312 for the animated ones.

**Reels versions** (nothing below is based on Instagram data, because I couldn't read any)
- Export a clean file without the TikTok watermark.
- Keep the hook text in the middle of the frame, away from the top and bottom edges where the Reels interface covers it.
- Pick a cover frame that shows the product.
- Tag the location (Fès or Sefrou).
- End with a question in the caption, because Reels tend to spread by sends and saves.

## Pillar 1: Stock, trucks, colours (your proven winners)

### 1. "Ce camion part pour [ville]"
- **Hook:** *Ce camion part pour Meknès ce matin* / *هاد الكاميو غادي لمكناس دابا*
- **Shots:** (1) truck loaded with bars at the store, (2) it pulls away, (3) city name appears on screen. No talking. 10–16s.
- **Caption/tags:** `Livraison du jour 🚚 #aluminium #maroc #fes #meknes #نجارة_الألمنيوم`
- **Why:** *Yours*: the truck video is your best at 14.7K views. Make it a weekly series and change the city each time, so you reach local audiences and show "livraison partout au Maroc" without saying it.

### 2. "Même profil, 6 teintes"
- **Hook:** *Même profil. 6 teintes. Laquelle choisit ton client ?* / *نفس البروفيل، 6 ألوان. أي لون غادي يختار الزبون؟*
- **Shots:** (1) walk down the aisle, (2) each colour labelled as it passes: noir, gris naturel, fauxbois, blanc 9010, gris platine, gris givré, (3) end on the same section in three colours side by side. 15–22s.
- **Why:** *Yours*: the stock walk got 8K. The side-by-side ending turns a passive tour into a choice people comment on.

### 3. "Arrivage"
- **Hook:** *Nouvel arrivage S88RP* / *وصلات السلعة الجديدة*
- **Shots:** a delivery being unloaded or a fresh bundle opened. Natural sound only. 8–12s.
- **Why:** *Yours/Niche*: raw stock footage is what your audience already watches. Very short silent clips are what the biggest Moroccan account (@proalumad) uses.

### 4. Folding mosquito net in three colours
- **Hook:** *Moustiquaire plissée : gris givré, blanc, quartz* / *الناموسية المطوية فـ 3 ألوان*
- **Shots:** hands pull the net across a window, one colour after another. 10–15s.
- **Why:** *Yours*: your May mosquito-net video had 25 shares on 2.7K views, which is a high share count for its reach, so people send it to someone who needs one.

## Pillar 2: Counter tips (your best engagement)

### 5. "Une astuce de 20 secondes" (dust fix, part 2)
- **Hook:** *Une astuce de 20 secondes qui évite les retours* / *حيلة د 20 ثانية كتحميك من المشاكل*
- **Shots:** same counter, same camera as your Sept video. Hold the profile and part, show **one** fix, finish with a question: *Et toi, tu fais comment ?* 20–30s.
- **Why:** *Yours*: the dust-fix video has the most saves (23), shares (26) and comments (11) of anything you've posted. The fixed setup lets viewers recognise the series. Run it weekly and pick topics from real customer questions.

### 6. S70P vs S90RP in your hands
- **Hook:** *Où est la rupture de pont thermique ?* / *فين كاينة قطعة العزل الحراري؟*
- **Shots:** two profiles side by side, finger on the thermal barrier, then the frame sizes (your site: S90 has a 90 mm frame). 20–30s.
- **Why:** *Yours/Untested*: your site says handling profiles "apprend en deux minutes ce qu'un catalogue met une heure à expliquer". This is that, on camera.

### 7. Quiz: "Quel profil porte 400 kg ?"
- **Hook:** *Lequel porte 400 kg par vantail ?* / *شمن بروفيل كيحمل 400 كيلو؟*
- **Shots:** four profile close-ups labelled S70P, S90RP, S88RP, S110P. Pause 2 seconds, reveal S110P. Pin the full list in a comment: S70P 80 kg, S90RP 160 kg, S88RP 200–300 kg, S110P 400 kg (from your site).
- **Why:** *Untested*: built-in comment bait with a checkable answer. Don't include the S125RP in the options, because your site flags its limits as needing validation.

### 8. "Pourquoi deux devis de profilés sont différents"
- **Hook:** *Même profilé, deux prix. Pourquoi ?* / *علاش جوج devis د البروفيلي مختلفين فالثمن؟*
- **Shots:** four quick text beats, one per factor from your FAQ: référence, quantité, finition, destination. Film the presenter at the counter. 20s.
- **Why:** *Niche*: captions about price/devis had a median of 12K views and a high save rate (0.38%, n=30). It needs no actual prices.

## Pillar 3: The 3D/AR tool (nobody else shows this)

### 9. "Il hésite ? Montre-lui chez lui"
- **Hook:** *Ton client n'arrive pas à se projeter ?* / *الزبون ما قادرش يتخيل؟ شوف هاد الحل*
- **Shots:** screen recording. Empty terrace, then a pergola appears at real size, rotates, changes colour. 15–25s.
- **CTA:** *Menuisiers : demandez votre lien.* It's for professional customers only, which makes it exclusive.
- **Why:** *Untested*, but it's the one thing no competitor in my sample shows, and your site gives the selling points (no app, loads in about a second on 4G).

### 10. Pergola in 30 seconds
- **Hook:** *Une pergola 4,20 × 6,00 m configurée en 30 secondes* / *بيرغولا فـ 30 ثانية*
- **Shots:** screen recording with a visible timer: size, colour, blades, LED, then the WhatsApp quote request arriving. 30s.
- **Why:** *Untested*: speed-run formats work as proof. The WhatsApp ending shows the benefit for the menuisier: a complete request with sizes and finish.

### 11. A menuisier and his client
- **Hook:** *Comment ce menuisier convainc son client avant la signature* / *كيفاش هاد النجار كيقنع الزبون قبل ما يوقع*
- **Shots:** ask a customer workshop to film themselves showing the tool to a client (their own logo and link). Tag them. 20–30s.
- **Why:** *Untested*: a customer's own video is more credible than yours, and they'll repost it. Start with 3 workshops.

### 13. The thinnest frame in the range
- **Hook:** *42 mm au centre. Le plus fin.* / *أنحف بروفيل*
- **Shots:** close-up of the S86RP centre section against a bright view. 8–10s.
- **Why:** *Niche*: same format as #12, based on your own spec (42 mm at the centre).

### 14. "400 kg… pour de vrai ?"
- **Hook:** *Un vantail de 400 kg ?* / *واش بصح كيحمل 400 كيلو؟*

- **Why:** *Untested*: spectacle plus a spec people remember.

## Pillar 5: Kitchens and dressings (the biggest audience)

### 15. Integrated handle, silent reveal
- **Hook:** *La poignée qu'on ne voit pas* / *المقبض لي ما كيبانش*
- **Shots:** a kitchen door opens by its integrated handle profile. No talking, no voiceover. 5–10s.
- **Tags:** `#cuisinealuminium #مطابخ_حديثة_المنيوم` plus a city.
- **Why:** *Niche*: kitchens have a median of ~30K views in this sample, and @proalumad's 7-second silent kitchen reveal reached 8.3M. Kitchen fabricators are your customers, so the people who watch are the people who buy.

### 16. One dressing, three finishes
- **Hook:** *Bicolore, effet bois ou anodisé ?* / *أي تشطيب كتفضل؟*
- **Shots:** the same profile in three finishes, wipe or swipe between them. 10–15s.
- **Why:** *Niche/Untested*: your site names these finishes for the S90RP, and a "pick one" question drives comments.

## Pillar 6: Humour, trust and replies

### 17. Skit: "un petit coulissant"
- **Hook:** *Le client veut un « petit coulissant »…* / *بغيت غير coulissant صغير…*
- **Shots:** the client holds out his hands, then a tape measure reads 4 metres. 15s, Darija, two people.
- **Why:** *Untested*: relatable humour about the menuisier's daily life. Try it 2 or 3 times, then keep it only if it earns shares.

### 18. "L'aluminium rouille ?"
- **Hook:** *L'aluminium rouille ?* / *واش الألمنيوم كيصدا؟*
- **Shots:** answer in one sentence, then show why (your exterior page: it forms a thin protective oxide layer and doesn't rust). 15–20s.
- **Why:** *Untested*: your site already has the explanation. Myth-busting is easy to share with a customer who asked the same thing.

### 19. "Touchez avant de commander" (Sefrou showroom)
- **Hook:** *Le showroom où tu peux tout comparer en main* / *جرّب بيدك قبل ما تطلب*
- **Shots:** walk through the Sefrou showroom, pick up two profiles, show opening hours (lundi–samedi, 9h–19h). 20–25s.
- **Why:** *Yours/Untested*: your top videos are shop-floor footage. This turns the showroom into the reason to visit.

### 20. Reply to a menuisier's comment
- **Hook:** the commenter's question on screen as a video reply, then your answer.
- **Shots:** 15–30s at the counter, one question, one answer.
- **Why:** *Niche*: your comment counts are 1–11, so any reply you give is also a reason for them to comment again. These cost almost nothing to film.
