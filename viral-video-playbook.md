# PROMPOLU — Viral video playbook (all 20 ideas, in detail)

Companion to `tiktok-reels-ideas.md` (the 20 ideas) and `prompolu-content-strategy.md` (the research).

**An honest note first.** Nobody can guarantee a video goes viral. TikTok doesn't publish how it ranks videos, and reach is lumpy: in your own account one video in ten passed 10K views, and in the 479-video niche sample the typical video got about 7K while a few got millions. What this playbook does is stack the odds: strong first second, short and clear content, something worth sharing or saving, and a reason to comment. Plan to post often, because the winners are hard to predict.

**How to read the cards.** *Yours* = from your own TikTok results. *Niche* = from the 479-video sample. *Untested* = a bet. Darija lines are drafts, so adapt them. Spec numbers (kg, mm, finishes) come from prompolu.com; check them against the STRUGAL sheets before you publish.

# Part 1: The rules that apply to every video

## 1.1 What your data says to stop doing
| Habit | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Opening on a logo, a "Hello!" or a blank screen | Your five animated videos had a median of 312 views (filmed: 4,546). Their cover frames were mostly a logo, "Hello!", or a near-blank canvas. |
| Landscape video | Your 21 Oct video is 1024×576. On a phone it plays as a thin strip. |
| Captions like "Retrouvez-nous sur notre site" | Your two biggest videos had hashtag-only captions. The weak ones used this line. |
| A phone number in the *caption* | Niche captions with a number: median 4.8K views vs 13.9K without (correlation). Put the number on the video, as your winners did. |
| Long gaps | 8 weeks without posting in May–July. |

## 1.2 The seven viral triggers (each video should hit at least two)
1. **Instant hook**: the first frame already shows something interesting, with a few words of text. No greeting, no logo, no "aujourd'hui je vais vous montrer".
2. **Rewatch**: a loop (last frame matches the first), a quick sequence people replay to catch, or a quiz where they check the answer.
3. **Share**: something a viewer sends to a specific person ("envoie ça à ton menuisier").
4. **Save**: a cheat sheet or a template people want to keep.
5. **Comment**: a binary choice, a question with a checkable answer, or a "what city next?".
6. **Local**: a city name, Darija, a familiar street. In your niche, Arabic captions had about 4× the median reach in Morocco (correlation).
7. **Real**: filmed with real hands, real stock, real trucks. This is what your audience already watches.

TikTok doesn't confirm how it weighs these, but watch time, shares, saves and comments are generally treated as the strongest signals.

## 1.3 Production standard
- **Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps.** Use 60–120 fps only for slow-motion shots.
- **Lock focus and exposure** (tap and hold) before recording, clean the lens, and use daylight.
- **Steady**: a small tripod, or elbows tucked into your body. No digital zoom.
- **Shot length**: 1–3 seconds for mechanism clips, 3–5 for explainers. Cut on movement.
- **Text**: 7 words or fewer, large, in the middle of the frame. Avoid the top and bottom edges (the app covers them). One font, high contrast.
- **Burned-in subtitles** for anything with speech. Many people scroll muted, and TikTok's data for your videos showed no auto-generated captions.
- **Sound**: original sound or ambient. Record close to the action. Your top 3 used original sound. (Your two licensed-music videos were also animated, so I can't separate the effects.)
- **Phone number** on the video, small, in the upper third.
- **Cover frame**: a product shot, not a logo.
- **Keep the edited file** from your editing app (no TikTok watermark) so you can upload the same file to Reels.

## 1.4 Posting checklist (every video)
1. **Caption**: one short line (Arabic or French) with a question when it fits, plus 3–5 tags: a city, a category and one Arabic tag. Drop #viral and #fyp.
2. **Pinned comment** with something useful (a list, a template, a question).
3. **First hour**: reply to every comment. Turn good questions into reply videos (idea #20).
4. **Reels**: same file, hook text in the middle of the frame, product cover, location tag (Fès or Sefrou), caption that ends with a question. Share to Stories. For partner videos, try the Instagram Collab post. *(No Instagram data was available, so these are general practice.)*
5. **WhatsApp**: add a one-line question to your greeting ("Vous nous avez connus par… ?") so you can count enquiries that came from social. That number matters more than views.

## 1.5 Benchmarks from the niche sample
- Typical share rate: **0.2–0.4% of views**. Typical save rate: **0.2–0.4%**.
- Your own median: **1,394 views**. A video at **3× your median within 48 hours** is a signal to make a sequel (see Part 4).

### 1. "Ce camion part pour [ville]"
**Triggers:** Rewatch · Local · Real  |  **Evidence:** Yours (your best video, 14.7K views, 127 likes, 20 shares)

**Why it can travel:** spectacle (a truck piled high with bars) plus a local reveal. It shows "livraison partout au Maroc" without saying it.

**Setup:** film from behind at street level. Fix the phone (wall, tripod or a helper standing still). Morning light. Ask someone to tighten the straps on camera. **Check:** nothing in frame that identifies a customer.

**Shot by shot (12–15s)**
| Time | Shot | Text / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | The loaded truck from behind, bars sticking out. | Hook text. Ambient engine. |
| 1.5–5 | A hand tightens the strap or slaps the tailgate. | — |
| 5–10 | The truck pulls away. The camera does not move. | — |
| 10–13 | Destination reveal. | "📍 Meknès" |
| 13–15 | Empty street (the loop point). | Phone number, small. |

**Hook A (statement):** *Ce camion part pour Meknès ce matin* / *هاد الكاميو غادي لمكناس دابا*
**Hook B (curiosity):** *Devine où part ce camion 🚚*, with the city hidden until 10s. People watch to the end and comment guesses.

**Edit:** no cuts during the drive-away; it's more satisfying as one take. Use a different city each time.

**Caption:** `Livraison du jour 🚚` · **Tags:** `#aluminium #maroc #fes #meknes #نجارة_الألمنيوم`

# Part 4: How to learn from the results

After 48 hours, compare each video to your own median (1,394 views) and to the niche rates (shares and saves 0.2–0.4%).

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **3× your median** | Make a sequel within 3 days, with the same hook structure and a new subject. |
| **Low views, high saves** | The idea is good but the hook is weak. Re-post with a new first second. |
| **High views, low shares** | It entertained but wasn't useful. Add a share prompt ("envoie à ton menuisier"). |
| **Comments with questions** | Answer with a reply video (#20) the same week. |
| **Under your median, twice in a row** | Retire that format for a month. |
| **WhatsApp enquiries from social** | This is the real score. Count them every week. |

After four weeks, rank the pillars by shares per 1,000 views and plan the next month around the top two.
